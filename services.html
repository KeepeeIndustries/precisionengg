Manufacturing services engineered for production lines

Four core capabilities under one roof — sand cores, VMC machining, impregnation and conventional machining — coordinated as a single accountable supply chain.

Service 01

Sand Core Manufacturing

Shell and cold-box sand cores that form the internal passages of complex castings — water jackets, oil galleries, intake tracts. Consistent sand chemistry and controlled shooting parameters mean cores that strip clean and cast clean.

  • Shell & cold-box processes
  • Core assemblies and painted cores
  • Dimensional stability across batches
  • Low gas-defect chemistry

Service 02

VMC Machining

Vertical machining centres with 3 and 4-axis capability, running aluminium and ferrous components to micron-level tolerances. Fixture design, CAM programming and in-process gauging are handled in-house.

  • Tolerances to ±0.01 mm
  • 3 & 4-axis machining with custom fixtures
  • In-process SPC and CMM verification
  • Serial production capacity

Service 03

Impregnation

Vacuum pressure impregnation permanently seals micro-porosity in castings and powder-metal parts. Components that would fail a leak test — or fail in the field — become pressure-tight without any dimensional change.

  • Vacuum + pressure sealant cycle
  • Leak-proof under operating pressure
  • Salvage of porous castings
  • No change to dimensions or finish
