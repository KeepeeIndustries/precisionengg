About Precision Engineering

A manufacturing partner built on accuracy, repeatability and trust

For over two decades, Precision Engineering has manufactured critical components for India's leading automobile manufacturers and Tier-1 suppliers.

We combine advanced machining infrastructure with deep process expertise across sand core manufacturing, VMC machining, impregnation and conventional machining — so you get dimensional accuracy on the first article and every article after it.

  • Single-roof capability — casting cores to finished, leak-tested components
  • Production-grade quality systems — inspection reports with every batch
  • Proven at OEM scale — trusted by names like Bajaj and Som Auto

What we do

Manufacturing services engineered for production lines

Four core capabilities, one accountable partner — from foundry cores to finished, sealed and inspected components.

Sand Core Manufacturing

Shell and cold-box sand cores that form complex internal passages in engine and housing castings — dimensionally stable, batch after batch.

Learn more

VMC Machining

3 and 4-axis vertical machining centres delivering micron-level tolerances on aluminium and ferrous components — milling, drilling, boring, tapping.

Learn more

Impregnation

Vacuum pressure impregnation that permanently seals micro-porosity in castings — making pressure-tight, leak-proof parts out of rejected ones.

Learn more

Conventional Machining

Turning, milling, drilling and grinding for prototypes, fixtures, tooling and low-volume runs — with the same inspection discipline as serial parts.

Learn more
