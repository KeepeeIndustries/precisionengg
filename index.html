About Precision Engineering

Built for consistency. Ready for scale.

Precision Engineering is a manufacturing business in Chitegaon, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, supplying sand cores, resin coated sand and machined components to automotive customers.

Most suppliers do one step and hand you to the next vendor. We control the whole chain — from the sand that goes into the core, to the machined, leak-tested part that leaves our gate. Fewer handoffs means fewer surprises.

  • Single-source accountability — one partner owns every stage
  • Process consistency — sand made in-house to controlled AFS and resin specs
  • Faster response — no waiting on outside sand suppliers or sub-vendors

What we do

Six capabilities, one accountable partner

Use one service or the whole chain — prototypes, development parts and volume production.

Resin Coated Sand

Silica resin coated sand made in our own cyclone-type plant — up to 700 MT a month, tested batch by batch for LOI, strength, grain size and gas.

Learn more

Core Making

Hot box and cold box sand cores for automotive castings, shot on 5 core shooters from sand we make ourselves. 4 more machines are on the way.

Learn more

VMC Machining

6 vertical machining centres, 3 of them with a 4th axis, for housings, brackets, flanges and pump bodies — with leak testing in-house.

Learn more

Impregnation

Vacuum impregnation that seals porosity in castings so they pass leak test — done on site, with batch records for every lot.

Learn more

Conventional Machining

14+ lathes, milling, drilling and boring machines for large parts, secondary operations, prototypes and breakdown jobs.

Learn more

Aluminium Gravity Die Casting Coming soon

Our new GDC unit — 2 stationary and 1 tilting machine — will add aluminium castings to the chain, made with our own cores.
