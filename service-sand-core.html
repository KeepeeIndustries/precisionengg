Sand cores that cast clean, strip clean and hold dimension

Shell and cold-box sand cores forming the internal passages of complex automotive castings — water jackets, oil galleries, intake tracts — with controlled chemistry and repeatable geometry.

Overview

The hidden geometry inside every casting

A casting is only as good as its cores. Ours are shot on automated shell and cold-box lines with tightly controlled sand grade, resin content and curing parameters — because a 0.3 mm shift inside a water jacket becomes a scrapped cylinder head at machining.

We supply individual cores, painted cores and assembled core packages ready for the mould line, with dimensional inspection records for every batch and gas-defect chemistry proven against your casting process.

Photo — cores exiting shell core machine

Process

How we run it

  1. Tooling & core-box review

    We study casting drawings and core-box tooling for parting lines, venting and print fits before committing to process parameters.

  2. Sand preparation & mix control

    Sand grade (AFS), resin and catalyst ratios are batch-controlled and logged — chemistry drift is caught before it reaches the machine.

  3. Core shooting

    Automated shell and cold-box machines shoot cores with controlled pressure, temperature and cycle time for uniform density.

  4. Curing, deflashing & coating

    Cores are cured, deflashed and coated as the casting process requires, preserving edge definition on print areas.

  5. Inspection & dispatch

    Gauging on critical dimensions, visual standards for surface defects, and packaging that protects cores in transit.
