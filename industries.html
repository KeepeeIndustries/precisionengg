Industries we serve

Our processes, quality systems and delivery discipline are shaped by the automotive industry — and trusted by manufacturers well beyond it.

Two-Wheeler Manufacturers

Serial supply of engine and transmission components for motorcycle and scooter OEMs — India's highest-volume, fastest-moving production lines.

  • Cylinder heads
  • Crankcase components
  • Clutch housings
  • Brake components

Passenger Vehicle OEMs

Precision castings and machined assemblies for powertrain, braking and chassis applications, delivered against JIT call-off schedules.

  • Valve bodies
  • Pump housings
  • Brackets & mounts
  • Manifolds

Commercial Vehicles

Components engineered for durability across millions of kilometres — machined and leak-tested for heavy-duty drivetrains.

  • Gearbox housings
  • Compressor bodies
  • Hydraulic blocks
  • Axle components

Agricultural Equipment

Tractor and farm-machinery parts built to survive dust, vibration and continuous load, season after season.

  • Transmission cases
  • Linkage components
  • PTO parts
