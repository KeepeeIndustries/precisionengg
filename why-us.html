Why choose Precision Engineering

Concrete reasons — not marketing language.

01 · Backward integration

We make our own sand. Input quality is controlled, not assumed. The chain from sand to component has no gap we cannot close.

02 · Single-partner accountability

Core making, machining and impregnation under one owner. No handoffs. One number to call. Problems get fixed.

03 · OEM-proven standards

We supply CIE Automotive — a zero-defect environment. Processes built to that bar apply to every customer we take on.

04 · Flexibility at scale

Conventional, CNC, core shop and RCS under one roof. Prototypes, development parts and volume production — no second vendor.

05 · Supply reliability

In-house RCS removes a key outside dependency. No sand supplier delays. Your schedule is our commitment.

06 · Scale-ready

4 more core shooters and a new aluminium GDC unit. We're building to grow with your volume — not just to fill today's PO.
