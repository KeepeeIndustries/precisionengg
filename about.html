Engineering trust into every component since 1998

Precision Engineering is a component manufacturing company serving automobile OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers and industrial clients with sand cores, precision machining and impregnation services.

Our story

From a two-machine workshop to an OEM-audited manufacturing plant

Precision Engineering began with a simple conviction: Indian manufacturing could match global standards of accuracy — if every process was measured, documented and improved relentlessly.

Today, that conviction runs a facility equipped with modern VMC machining centres, automated core-shooting lines and a vacuum impregnation plant. We serve some of the country's most demanding automotive nameplates, holding tolerances measured in microns across serial production volumes.

What hasn't changed is how we work: engineers who study your drawing before quoting, quality teams who inspect what the process already proved, and delivery schedules that survive contact with reality.

What we stand for

Three principles behind every part we ship

Precision first

Accuracy is not a premium option — it is the baseline. Every process is qualified against the tightest tolerance it must hold, then monitored so it keeps holding it.

Accountability

One partner, one point of responsibility. From core to finished component, if we accepted the drawing, we own the outcome — including the schedule.

Continuous improvement

Rejection data, cycle times and customer feedback feed a standing improvement loop. The plant you audit next year should be better than the one you audited this year.

Milestones

A quarter century of steady, deliberate growth

  1. 1998

    Founded as a machining workshop

    Precision Engineering opens with conventional lathes and a single industrial client.

  2. 2004

    First automotive OEM order

    Serial supply of machined engine components begins for a two-wheeler manufacturer.

  3. 2009

    Sand core division commissioned

    Shell and cold-box core-shooting lines bring foundry-stage capability in-house.

  4. 2014

    VMC machining cell expansion

    Multi-axis vertical machining centres raise capacity and tolerance capability to ±0.01 mm.

  5. 2019

    Vacuum impregnation plant installed

    Leak-proofing of porous castings closes the loop on end-to-end component supply.

  6. 2024

    500+ components in serial production

    Supplying OEMs and Tier-1s across two-wheeler, passenger vehicle and industrial segments.
