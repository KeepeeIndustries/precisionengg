VMC machining with inspection built into every run

6 vertical machining centres — 3 with a 4th axis — plus in-house leak testing and impregnation for parts that must not leak.

Overview

Machined parts that match your drawing — batch after batch

Our machining cell runs 6 vertical machining centres. Three of them run a 4th axis, so more faces can be machined in one setup — better accuracy and less handling.

We machine part families such as housings, brackets, flanges and pump bodies. Setups are fixture-based to reduce operator variation, and tooling is tracked job by job.

Leak testing (LT) and ball bearing testing (BT) are done in-house, and leak-critical parts can go straight to our impregnation plant — no outside vendor in between.

Process discipline

How every job runs

  1. First-off inspection

    Every job starts with a full dimensional check before the batch runs.

  2. In-process gauging

    Critical dimensions checked at fixed intervals during the run.

  3. Fixture repeatability

    Setups validated before production is released.

  4. Operator SOPs

    Step-by-step job cards reduce setup variation between shifts.

  5. Non-conformance log

    Rejects tracked by job, machine and operator — and fixed at the root.
