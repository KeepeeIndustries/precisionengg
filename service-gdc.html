Aluminium gravity die casting — the next link in our chain

We are setting up a GDC unit with 2 stationary and 1 tilting machine, so cores, castings, machining and leak-proofing can all come from one partner.

What's coming

Adding aluminium castings to the chain

Gravity die casting (GDC) means pouring molten aluminium into a reusable steel die, using gravity to fill it. Compared with sand casting, it gives denser metal, a better surface and more repeatable dimensions — ideal for medium-to-high volume automotive parts.

We are setting up a GDC unit at Chitegaon with 2 stationary and 1 tilting GDC machine. The castings will use cores from our own core shop — made from our own resin coated sand — and can be machined, leak tested and impregnated in the same plant.

That means one partner, from sand to a finished, leak-tested aluminium component.

Machines

Two ways to pour — matched to the part

2×

Stationary GDC machines

The die stays fixed and metal is poured straight in. Simple, fast and robust — well suited to parts with straightforward shapes and steady volumes.

  • Simpler geometries
  • Quick die changes
  • Good for steady volumes
1×

Tilting GDC machine

The die tilts while the metal is poured, so aluminium flows in smoothly instead of splashing. Less turbulence means fewer oxides and less gas porosity.

  • Complex and cored parts
  • Leak-critical castings
  • Cleaner metal, fewer defects
